# Five-Minute Demo Script

1. Open the landing page and highlight the training-focused SOC aesthetic.
2. Select the Account Compromise scenario and explain that the data is fictional and simulated.
3. Review the initial alert, event stream, and status cards in the command center.
4. Click the timeline entries and inspect the related log evidence.
5. Choose an investigation action and explain the score impact and educational rationale.
6. Open the asset and user investigation panels to highlight the relationship map.
7. Trigger the AI assistant and show the deterministic explanation mode.
8. View the final report and analytics panel for score and recommendation feedback.
9. Reset the scenario and switch to Demo Mode to show the live event experience.
10. Conclude with the importance of forensic evidence and safe containment decisions.

This flow is designed to take approximately 3–5 minutes and works without internet access after local installation.
